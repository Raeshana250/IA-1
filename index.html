Critical Essay: Use LLMs as a TooL, Not a Crutch

LLMs stands for Large Language Models, which is an artificial intelligence system capable of undestanding massive amounts of data and generating human lanaguage. As a student, the statement "LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch" means that we should use AI to expand human intelligence, like a boost rather than replacing our cognitive process of how we truly learn or problem solve. Relying on AI to generate finished solutions leads to cognitive decline, leaving students unprepared for real-world problem solving.

When used properly like a tool, students can use them to break down parts of their notes that have difficult concepts, generate quizzes or worksheets to practice, or even proofread their essays for clarity. While using it as a crutch, means we're outsourcing our critical thinking. For instance, generating a whole essay for an assignment or a whole code eliminates the process of critical thinking so it sticks for the long-term. The goal is to work with AI rather than replace our individual thoughts.

Reflection

These titles are based off two chapters from the book The Richest Man in Babylon" by George S Clason.

7 Cures for a Lean Purse

This chapter outlines Arkad's fundamental financial principles to help citizens build personal wealth. He argues that before wealth can be accrued, a person must first have a strong and clear desire to master their skills and simultaneously learn. He states that "necessary expenses" naturally scale up to meet your income unless knowingly restricted. He also stated the phrase "Start thy purse to fattening", which means save at least 10% of everything you earn, controlling expense and making gold multiple by budgeting. As a second year student, these views can align with my life for future earning. As a student, our lives can be unpredictable with part-time income or internships. With the second cure, it talks about "Control thy expenditures" which means try to make goals or a budget so you don't overspend on things you don't need, subscriptions services, eating-out, etc. Try to make your expenses fit in your available means. The seventh cure talks about "Increase thy means to earn" which stands for making connections, prioritizing projects and learning new information to better your knowledge and even seeking mentorship to improve your skills.


5 Laws of Gold

This chapter talks about the contrasts between wisdom and gold. He argues, "If man had a choice, would they pick wisdon or gold." He states, "They would ignore the wisdom and waste the gold." He conclude that, "Gold is reserved for those who know its laws ad abide by them." The first law talks about how gold will multiple the sooner its set up. It demonstrates that wealth can only be secure with trusted and oversight owners who seek expert advice and avoid unfamiliar and suspicious ventures. The third and fourth laws warns us about investing our money in things we do not fully understand or relying on advice from amateurs.

What I takeaway from these chapters is that financial freedom is not a result of luck. It takes discipline, goals/budget and saving.
